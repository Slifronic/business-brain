## 1. Never claim what we can't prove

At idea stage, the only proven results are the founder's FBLA placements. No invented users, revenue, partners, or impact numbers — anywhere, ever.
**Test:** every number and named result in any output traces to a real source in this brain.

## 2. Volunteers are users, not product

Sycamore is sold to businesses, but if the experience doesn't genuinely build volunteers' skills, the business has no reason to exist.
**Test:** every feature or pitch answers "what does the volunteer gain?" before "what does the buyer get?"

## 3. Ship small, learn fast

Pre-launch stage means speed of learning beats polish. Imperfect drafts today beat perfect drafts never.
**Test:** any plan includes the smallest version that puts something in front of a real person this week.

## 4. Dated facts or no facts

The business will change its mind. Every recorded fact, decision, and metric carries a date.
**Test:** brain notes have the date in the filename; context files carry a "last updated" line.

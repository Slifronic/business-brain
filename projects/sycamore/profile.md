# Sycamore — Project Profile

_Last updated: 2026-07-07. Source: founder brain dumps (voice). Flagship project._

## One-liner

A B2B volunteering platform that aims to become the standardized system for volunteering — what College Board is to standardized testing, Sycamore is to volunteer credentialing.

## Stage

Prototype idea (confirmed 2026-07-07). The dashboard described below is spec/pitch material, not a built product. Next milestone: "make it off the pitch into form" — turn the pitch into a working prototype.

## The product

**Volunteer side (end users):**
- Badges that act as certifications
- A timeline of achievements: how many badges earned, when, which conditions and requirements were fulfilled, and which organizations they worked with

**Buyer side (the dashboard businesses pay for):**
- Tracks the data volunteers/workers accomplish
- A score covering: volunteer retention, coordinator efficiency, event completion
- A drop-down system for nonprofits and schools: assign volunteers to roles in one click, verify hours and work done

## Buyers

- Nonprofits
- Schools
- Corporations (motivated by CSR — corporate social responsibility reporting)

## Positioning

- Untapped market: founder believes this model has not been done before.
- Long-term ambition: national recognition as *the* standard for volunteering, the way College Board standardized testing.

# Sycamore — Project Profile

_Last updated: 2026-07-07. Sources: founder brain dumps (2026-07-07) + Google Drive doc "SYCAMORE" (last edited 2026-06-07, archived at `sources/2026-06-07_sycamore-pitch-doc_gdrive-export.md`)._

## One-liner

A volunteering platform that turns service into verified, portable career credentials — aiming to become the standard for volunteering the way College Board is for testing. Brand acronym: **MORE** — Mastery, Opportunity, Recognition, Empowerment.

## Stage

Prototype idea; pitch is well-developed, product not built. Next milestone: "make it off the pitch into form."
**Proof of concept:** a live program run with **Our City Forest** using the PBL chapter's community service project (CSP) data — cited in the pitch as Index 78 / Gold, 609 volunteer hours, 203 active volunteers.

## The ecosystem (four segments, one data spine)

One platform, one badge library, one Sycamore Index. Volunteers generate the data; organizations pay for the proof.

### Volunteers — free forever (strategic, not a discount)
- **Badge Portfolio:** universal, standardized badges (Resource Logistics, Community Outreach, Event Coordination, Team Leadership, Strategic Communication, Program Management…). Same badge means the same thing everywhere.
- Coordinator assigns role per event (general volunteer → team lead → event coordinator → program manager); post-event 2-minute assessments verify work; thresholds auto-issue badges. Continuous engagement required — no participation stickers.
- Service timeline + progress-to-next-badge; exportable profile for resume/LinkedIn/college apps. Credential follows the person.
- No leaderboards; volunteers don't see other profiles.
- **Volunteer Link (Year 3-4):** invitation-only 8-12 week accelerator for standout volunteers (project management, data analysis, stakeholder management, business cases). Free for volunteers; corporate sponsors fund cohorts for first-access recruiting.

### Nonprofits — $0 to $199/mo
- Dashboard around the **Sycamore Index** (0-100): retention rate (attendance patterns) + efficiency ratio (coordinator time per 100 volunteer hours) + mission completion (post-event confirmations).
- Churn warnings ~3 weeks before a volunteer disappears; churn-cause analysis (e.g., "no role progression within 90 days → 3x churn").
- **Certification tiers** (Platinum/Gold/Silver/Bronze) for grant applications; benchmarking against peers.
- Tier structure in product spec: Free (<25 volunteers), Starter $49/mo (25-100, adds certification), Growth $149/mo (100-500, adds peer benchmarking + funder reports). Priced ~45-80% below VolunteerHub ($143-288/mo) — deliberately cheap; mission conflict otherwise.

### Schools — $99 to $499/mo
- Identical product; the context differs: auto-verification kills paper sign-in sheets (~15 hrs/week coordinator time saved), student badge portfolios for college apps, admin-level aggregate reporting.
- **The College Board dynamic:** get colleges to value the credential → schools must adopt or disadvantage their students. Standard spreads through credential pressure, not sales.

### Corporations — the biggest value-add (~$10 back per $1 spent)
- Tier 1: Index measurement subscription, $499/mo — quarterly reports in CFO/HR language (engagement correlation, competency mapping, ROI).
- Tier 2: managed service — Sycamore designs and runs a ~3-month leadership program through real community service with nonprofit partners, replacing ~$85k ballroom summits. - Tier 3: Volunteer Link pipeline access, $25k-50k per cohort (Year 3-4).
- Rationale: corporations spend $40B/yr on leadership training; 70-20-10 model says 70% of learning is hands-on, summits deliver the 10%.

## Example badges (with real requirements)

- **Resource Logistics Organizer** (HS students): 15 hrs sorting/organizing + documented organizing scheme.
- **Social Media Strategist** (college): 40+ hrs, 3+ deliverables, signed engagement report (pre/post metrics).
- **Pro Bono Strategy Consultant** (professionals): 40+ hr skills project signed off as "Implemented and Actionable" by nonprofit director.
- **Urban Canopy Practitioner** / **Community Impact Project Lead** (PBL CSP-specific): field hours, technique documentation, timestamped photos, 1-month follow-up / project blueprint, success validation form.

## Go-to-market (2 phases)

1. **Proof before selling:** free trials → every trial becomes a case study; student ambassadors (Key Club, NHS, "Sycamore Corps"); LinkedIn to project managers/event coordinators; PBL CSP dataset as founding proof.
2. **Leverage:** case studies + Index data → corporate pitch; College Board strategy for schools; campus-by-campus expansion (De Anza → SJSU → SCU → UCSC), not UC-system-level deals.
- Known plan weaknesses (from embedded marketing critique): buyer/ICP under-defined (best first ICP: volunteer coordinators at mid-sized nonprofits), conversion funnel missing, growth numbers aggressive.

# Sycamore — History

Dated entries: what happened, what was decided, what to remember.

## Fall 2025 (backfilled 2026-07-07 from Drive)

- Sycamore born as "Serve Your Community Always (Syca)" — consumer gamification app with collectible badges and a Service Garden. Applied to De Anza Founders' Forum (teammate: Le Phuoc). Also developed as a PBL project.
- Hard critique session exposed the flaws (friction > motivation, unethical nonprofit charging, weak financials) → pivoted toward verification/operations SaaS with gamification as retention layer. See [[2026-07-07_sycamore-origin-and-pivot]].

## By 2026-06-07 (backfilled from Drive pitch doc)

- Pitch matured into the four-segment ecosystem: Badge Portfolio, Sycamore Index, certification tiers, corporate leadership programs, Volunteer Link accelerator.
- Live proof of concept run with Our City Forest using PBL chapter CSP data.

## 2026-07-07

- Project entered the second brain as the flagship project during initial brain-dump sessions.
- Positioning locked: "College Board of volunteering" — standardized volunteer credentialing. See brain note [[2026-07-07_sycamore-positioning-standardized-volunteering]].
- Product concept articulated: volunteer badges-as-certifications + achievement timeline; buyer dashboard with retention/efficiency/completion score and one-click role assignment + hour verification for nonprofits and schools.
- Stage confirmed: prototype idea only — dashboard is pitch/spec, nothing built. Next milestone: "make it off the pitch into form."

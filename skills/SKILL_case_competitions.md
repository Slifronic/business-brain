# SKILL: Business Case Competitions (FBLA/PBL)

_Source: Bryant's own PBL notes sheet ("PBL Bryant Vo, Notes Sheet", Google Doc, entries dated Oct 2025; mined 2026-07-07). This is competition-tested knowledge — Bryant placed 1st twice at CA state level and 2nd at NLC nationals. Load for: case prep, pitch decks, competition practice, and any Sycamore pitch work._

## What a case is

"What would you do if you were the decision-maker?" Anatomy: Context → Problems → Data (know ALL the numbers) → Objectives → Constraints → Decision (needs a firm end result).

## The FBLA Case Comp Pyramid (what wins)

1. **Storytelling and presence** — why it matters, why it's correct, explained well
2. **Analytical rigor** — the financial connection
3. **Structured thinking**

Judged on five rubric principles: Problem Identification, Analytical Support, Creativity & Feasibility, Organization & Communication, Professional Presence.

## The working method: Analyze → Ideate → Decide → Deliver

**Analyze:** read once fast for story, then deeply for numbers. Identify the core decision, key stakeholders, measurable objectives. Tools: SWOT (threats = always include competitors), Problem Tree (causes → focal problem → effects), 5 Whys, Ohmae's 3 C's (Customers, Corporation, Competitors), Porter's 5 Forces, PESTLE.

**Ideate:** many ideas, MECE (mutually exclusive, collectively exhaustive). Practicality first, flash second.

**Decide:** impact × feasibility (pick high/high), decision matrix, quick quantitative reasoning (cost, risk).

**Deliver:** logic → story. Situation → Problem → Recommendation → Implementation → Result. End with measurable impact.

## The 5-part story arc (with calibration examples)

1. Situation — what's happening now ("sales decreased 15%")
2. Problem — what's wrong and the root cause ("low customer retention")
3. Recommendation — what to do ("launch loyalty rewards program")
4. Implementation — how ("pilot in 3 cities Q1 2025")
5. Impact — why it matters ("expected +12% revenue lift")

Speech rules: solution statement FIRST. Objectives SMART. Address counterarguments and why they fail. Sources in a textbox at the end of each slide, and say them in the speech — a cited number won't get pushed in Q&A.

## Financial toolkit (formulas judges expect)

- **NPV:** discount each future cash flow to present value, sum, subtract initial outlay. NPV > 0 → accept.
- **IRR:** discount rate where NPV = 0 (`=IRR(range)` in Excel).
- **Payback period:** initial investment ÷ annual cash inflow. Shorter = quicker risk recovery.
- **Gross margin %:** (gross profit ÷ revenue) × 100; gross profit = revenue − COGS.
- **ROA:** net income ÷ total assets. **ROE:** net income ÷ shareholders' equity.
- **Current ratio:** current assets ÷ current liabilities (>1 healthy). **Quick ratio:** (cash + AR) ÷ current liabilities (≥1 = covers liabilities without selling inventory).
- **Breakeven:** Q = fixed costs ÷ (price − variable cost).
- **Sensitivity analysis:** Δoutput% ÷ Δinput%; test ±10% swings to find the risk levers.
- **Diagnostic accounting:** common-size analysis (item ÷ total sales × 100), variance vs budget, cash-flow segmentation (operating + investing + financing — healthy firms fund growth from operations).
- **Ethics checklist:** document sources, show assumptions, cross-check sums, attach an assumptions-and-sources sheet.
- Market sizing heuristic used in comp: take ~10% of the market size for a go-to-market claim.

## Strategy tools for differentiation sections

Customer Journey Map (Awareness → Consideration → Purchase → Service → Loyalty), Value Proposition Canvas (pains/gains/jobs vs relievers/creators), Blue Ocean (Raise / Reduce / Eliminate / Create), Brand Pyramid (features → functional benefits → emotional benefits → persona → brand idea).

## Deck and delivery rules

- One headline per slide, stating the insight. ≤25 words of text per slide. Visual hierarchy: Title > Graphic > Takeaway. Data visuals, never clip-art or text walls.
- Body: balanced open stance; eye contact 3–5 seconds per judge; purposeful gestures; slow pace; **pause after numbers**.
- Q&A: answer in one sentence → evidence → impact. If unsure, acknowledge and redirect. Never interrupt.
- Behavioral answers: STAR method.

## Quality checklist (before any pitch or case deliverable ships)

- [ ] Solution statement appears before the analysis
- [ ] Every number has a stated source next to it
- [ ] Financial section includes at least one of: NPV/payback/breakeven/margin
- [ ] Counterarguments addressed explicitly
- [ ] Slides pass the ≤25-word rule with one insight headline each
- [ ] Ends on measurable impact

# Decision: Sycamore positions as the standard for volunteering (College Board analogy)

Date: 2026-07-07

**Decided:** Sycamore's north-star positioning is "standardized volunteering" — become the nationally recognized standard for volunteer credentialing, the way College Board is the standard for standardized testing.

**Because:** The market is untapped (founder believes no one has built B2B volunteer credentialing tied to profession-relevant skills), and a standards play compounds: badges only matter as certifications if one system issues them widely.

**Implication:** Product choices should favor legitimacy and verifiability (verified hours, requirement-based badges, buyer-side scoring) over social-app features.

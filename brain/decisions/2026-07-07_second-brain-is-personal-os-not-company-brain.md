# Decision: The second brain is a personal OS, not a company brain

Date: 2026-07-07

**Decided:** This brain serves Bryant as an individual across every project he pursues, rather than serving one business. The `clients/` layer from the original blueprint became `projects/`, one folder per project.

**Because:** Bryant has no operating business yet and multiple interests; [[sycamore]] is simply the biggest and most thought-out project. Structuring the brain around one company would force a restructure the moment a second project starts.

**Implication:** `context/` describes Bryant (voice, values, background); each project's specifics live in `projects/{name}/`.
